## 2. What Bonfire is

Bonfire is a **heuristic helper**. It looks for patterns on a webpage (hidden text, urgency language, common checkout tricks, and similar signals) and may clean some clipboard text.

It is **not**:

- an antivirus, firewall, or professional security audit
- a guarantee that a site is safe, honest, or legal
- a consumer-protection, fraud, or shopping advice service
- a detector that can prove text was or was not written by a human or an AI
- affiliated with Google, Chrome, OpenAI, Anthropic, or any site it scans
- a substitute for reading a page, a contract, or a checkout flow yourself

Scores can be wrong. Traps can be missed. Harmless pages can be flagged. The practice demo is **fiction** and not a real store.
